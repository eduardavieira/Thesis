\subsection{Confidence Intervals}
\label{ap:confidence_intervals}

The tables below give the 95\% confidence interval of the mean of each metric, computed with the $t$ distribution over the runs or folds of each protocol, $\bar{x} \pm t_{0.975,\,n-1}\, s / \sqrt{n}$. Under GroupKFold and leave-one-subject-out the folds are treated as independent samples, which is the usual approximation; their training sets overlap. Where the interval extends beyond the range of the metric it is truncated at 0 or at its maximum. Values reported from a single evaluation, such as the confusion matrix and the explainability analysis of Model G, have no interval.
